Most coffee is old before you open it.

Roasted beans taste their best for a few weeks. Supermarket shelves do not run on that timetable.

  • No roast date on the bag

    A best-before date can be a year away. It tells you nothing about when the beans were roasted.

  • Pre-ground goes flat

    Ground coffee loses its aroma much faster than whole beans. By the second week the cup tastes dull.

  • Running out at the worst time

    You find the empty bag on a Monday morning. A subscription sends the next one before that happens.

Questions

When will my coffee ship?

We roast every Monday and ship every Wednesday with tracking. Orders placed by Sunday at 11:59 pm go into that Monday's roast. Anything later joins the following week.

Should I buy whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longer. If you do not own a grinder, pick the grind for your brewer and we grind it on packing day, the day before it ships.

How long does the coffee stay fresh?

It tastes best between 7 and 30 days after the roast date printed on the bag. Keep it sealed in the bag, away from light and heat. The fridge is not a good place for it.

Which coffee should I pick for espresso?

Start with Long Table. It is roasted a little darker and holds up well with milk. Colombia Huila also makes a sweet espresso on its own.

Can I pause or cancel a subscription?

Yes, with no fee. Email hello@lowfield.example before the Sunday cut-off and we will skip or stop the next roast. A self-serve account page is coming soon.

What if something is wrong with my order?

Email us with your order number. If a bag arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, we will send a new one in the next roast.
